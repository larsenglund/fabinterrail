FabInterrail · Phase 0 · Design direction

The timetable is the interface.

Four key screens in the proposed design system: paper & ink with hairline rules, heavy tabular numerals for times and money, one signal amber accent, and indigo reserved for night trains. Typography is the native platform face (SF Pro / Roboto in the app), because the app should feel like the phone it runs on.

Flip the viewer's theme toggle to see night-train mode — a designed dark theme, not an inversion. Tab bars in the frames are clickable.
